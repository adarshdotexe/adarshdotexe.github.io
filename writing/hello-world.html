Wishing myself a very happy birthday

Shivers run down my spine as I inch closer to finally having my own personal website. What better day to do it than on my birthday. Why do I want one? Well… This will not only serve as my portfolio website, hosting most of my technical projects, but also as a place for me to express myself to strangers on the internet, or to simply rant at the vast nothingness of the internet.

Why should you keep an eye on my website? Well… you shouldn't. I made this to flex a little to my classmates and hopefully have something good looking on my CV. Although I plan to use this space to share projects and technical tutorials in the future, I will most likely take a break now that this is finally working.

If you know me personally and want to read my thoughts on topics I find interesting enough to write a complete blog post about, feel free to stick around. And while you're here, drop me a message if you have written something and want me to put it on my website. Also, here's a random quote that I like:

Cleverness, he thinks, gets you nowhere; it is only the stupid, the brilliantly stupid, who will inherit the earth.
— Ian McGuire

For the people reading this in the hopes of building something similar, I might share a tutorial when I find the time and motivation. Until then, feel free to fork my repository on GitHub and make your own changes. Honestly, most of the credit for this website goes to the beautiful template Breezy CV, which I simply modified to my liking.

If you have made it this far, you must be really bored reading the personal blog of a simple engineering student, but hey, thanks for sticking around, and I hope you are doing well. Feel free to drop me a message, and share this with your friends to show how cool I am :P
