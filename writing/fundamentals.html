Fundamentals, Programming Fundamentals

Old post from 2022. Registrations closed long ago.

I take this oppurtunity to formally invite student of National Institute of Andhra Pradesh to join CS007 Fundamentals, Programming Fundamentals where we will work to learn the most important skills needed in order to become a sucessful software developer. I will try my best to enculcate a feeling of excitement and acomplishment that I personally experience while coding but at the end of the day if spending time sitting in front of a screen is not something you see yourself doing then this is not for you.

Some of the topics covered during the classes include: Basics of C, Data Structures and Algorithms, Integrated Developement Environments, How to find resources online, Oppurtunities in the future, personal project ideas, discussions, soft skills, presentation and collaboration, github and basics of linux and open source projects, etc.

The classes shall be held thrice a week on Wednesdays, Thursdays and Fridays from 5:15 PM to 6:15 PM but you will end up hating it if you are not willing to code outside of class hours.

I will only accept the 45–60 students (maximum) during the initial phase to see if I am able to achieve the course objectives while keeping the classes interesting and informative. The selections for those 50 positions will be done on a first come first serve basis and I will try and give as much individual attention to skill developement and personal growth as possible.

For any concerns or queries feel free to contact me either using the contact form on my website or send me a hello via Whatsapp or E-mail

I would like to thank The Computer Science and Engineering Association of NIT Andhra Pradesh and Dr Himabindu, Head of Department, Computer Science and Engineering Department NIT Andhra Pradesh for their constant motivation and for supporting and approving my ideas and allowing me to turn a vision of mine into a reality.

Do check out other parts of the website, every visit counts (literally), and contact me if you wish to add your testimonial to the website.

If you have made it this far also let me know where you heard about this page in the comments section of the google form, I am not promoting the registration from except for sharing it in the juniors group to know how important posters truly are for engagement if at all.
