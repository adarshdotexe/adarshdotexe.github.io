Fundamentals, programming fundamentals

From the archive: registrations for this course closed long ago.

I take this opportunity to formally invite students of the National Institute of Technology Andhra Pradesh to join CS007 Fundamentals, Programming Fundamentals, where we will learn the most important skills needed to become a successful software developer. I will try my best to instil the excitement and accomplishment I personally feel while coding, but at the end of the day, if spending time in front of a screen is not something you see yourself doing, this is not for you.

Topics include the basics of C, data structures and algorithms, integrated development environments, how to find resources online, opportunities in the future, personal project ideas, discussions, soft skills, presentation and collaboration, GitHub, and the basics of Linux and open source.

Classes will be held three times a week, on Wednesdays, Thursdays and Fridays from 5:15 PM to 6:15 PM, but you will end up hating it if you are not willing to code outside class hours.

I will only accept 45–60 students in the initial phase, to see whether I can meet the course objectives while keeping the classes interesting and informative. Seats go first come, first served, and I will try to give as much individual attention to skill development and personal growth as possible.

I would like to thank the Computer Science and Engineering Association of NIT Andhra Pradesh and Dr Himabindu, Head of the Computer Science and Engineering Department, for their constant motivation, and for supporting and approving my ideas and letting me turn a vision of mine into reality.
