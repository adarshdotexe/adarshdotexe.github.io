Portfolio

  1. NVIDIA

    The power model

    The web app and the Python client share one engine.

    A run is one what-if: change a clock or a voltage and get the power back for every block and rail. Each what-if becomes a tiny program that only redoes the steps whose inputs changed, and a slower reference engine checks its answers. It’s TypeScript on Bun and SvelteKit. I rewrote it from scratch in about 60 days (1,044 commits).

    I can’t show the internals (it’s NVIDIA stuff), but the 1 min 55 s and the 60 µs are from my own runs.

  2. NVIDIA

    PACE

    A chatbot over our team’s docs. Twelve interns ramped up on it and rated it 9.5/10.

    PACE stands for Power Architecture Chat Engine. It answers questions from our codebase and Confluence pages, using OpenWebUI, ChromaDB, LiteLLM and FastAPI, and I built it so agent workflows could sit on top later. When it gave a wrong answer, it was usually because the page behind it was outdated, so we fixed the pages too. It won NTech 2025, NVIDIA’s internal paper competition.

  3. 2026

    Lokasu

    My own studio. lokasu.com and its free Launch Check are live.

    I designed and coded lokasu.com in SvelteKit and Three.js. Thirteen 3D models share the same 512 parts and morph from one to the next, and your cursor pushes them around. Launch Check runs on a Cloudflare Worker. Paste a link and in under 20 seconds it tells you what will break when you share it, like a missing preview image or a dead link. It doesn’t need an account.

    Two more are in progress and private for now. Creator Studio reads your own notes and saved links, suggests post ideas that cite their sources, and publishes nothing until you approve the exact text. Home Canvas helps people in India furnish a new flat on its actual floor plan before they move in.

  4. 2026

    Vilje Tech

    AI and automation for Nordic companies. I build ViljePDF, ViljeCAD and Inwoice.

    ViljePDF reads engineering drawings that have no text layer, shows every value next to the spot it came from so a person can check it, and compares two revisions line by line. It runs locally on ONNX Runtime with no cloud calls. On held-out public datasets it scores F1 0.99 on GD&T frames and parts lists.

    ViljeCAD turns drawing PDFs into a 3D model, a floor plan, a bill of materials and an IFC file. The code measures every line on the sheet and the AI model decides what each line means. Its accuracy is checked against the ink on the drawing, down to 0.01 mm.

    Inwoice takes Swedish supplier invoices from the inbox to Fortnox or Visma. AI reads each invoice, and bookkeeping rules plus a person’s approval decide what gets posted. It’s built against a spec of 113 features, and every one of them is tied to a test.

  5. College

    StarCraft II paper

    My final-year project on StarCraft II tactics, published by Springer.

    Instead of learning the game one click at a time, our agent chose between bigger tactics, using a knowledge-infused policy gradient with UCB. Across five mini-games it beat DeepMind’s baseline agent at gathering resources and training units, and lost badly in fights. Written at NIT Andhra Pradesh and published in the ICTIS 2025 proceedings in January 2026.
